Help & About

What this is · What it is not
Read Me First

What is AMA?

AMA stands for Ask Morpheus Alliance. It’s a creative framework for seekers who like to ask “Who am I?”, “Where am I going?”, “How am I?”, and “What am I to become?”.

This app blends simple numerology, a Mayan-style date key, and light Qur’an study into one experience. It’s here to educate and entertain – not to judge anyone’s faith or replace real scholarship.

What this app is not

• Not a fatwa site
• Not a replacement for scholars, imams, or teachers
• Not a final word on religion

Treat everything here as a conversation starter and a study companion. If you have serious spiritual or legal questions, speak to a qualified religious teacher or imam.

Lifespan: Period Cycle

The Three Great Stages of Your Life
Period Energy Chart

Your life is seen as three major "Period Cycles," each lasting for roughly 25-30 years. Each period is defined by a single-digit number (from your birth date) that represents the central theme and lessons of that stage. This is based on the vibrational triangle from your CPAL-R.

Calculate your CPAL-R in the Birth Code Room to see your Period Cycle chart.

Period Energy Explanations

Each number represents a core energy or set of lessons for that Period of your life.

Explanations will appear once your profile is calculated.

Lifespan: Pinnacle Cycle

The Four Clouds of Development
Pinnacle Cloud Chart

Pinnacles are four distinct periods of development (or "Clouds") that you experience. Each Pinnacle has a specific duration and a number that represents the energy, opportunities, and challenges of that time. Calculating them helps you understand what to focus on during each stage.

Calculate your CPAL-R in the Birth Code Room to see your Pinnacle Cycle chart.

Pinnacle Energy Explanations

Each number represents the core theme for that Pinnacle of your life.

Explanations will appear once your profile is calculated.

Lifespan: Life Challenge

The Four Hurdles on Your Path
Challenge Energy Chart

Your Life Challenges are not punishments; they are specific hurdles or weaknesses you are here to overcome and master. They are calculated by finding the *difference* between the points of your vibrational triangle. Mastering them brings wisdom and strength.

Calculate your CPAL-R in the Birth Code Room to see your Life Challenge chart.

Challenge Energy Explanations

Each number represents a core lesson. Your 1st Challenge is active in your youth, the 2nd in adulthood, and the 3rd & 4th are present throughout your life.

Explanations will appear once your profile is calculated.
